# Oche Support

Oche is a darts score keeper for iPhone and iPad, with X01 and Cricket scoring, practice modes, and player stats.

## Contact

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email **goldfinchdevs@gmail.com** and include your device model and iOS version. I aim to reply within a few days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I restore my Oche Pro purchase?**
Open any locked Pro feature, tap **Unlock Oche Pro**, then tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

**What does Oche Pro include?**
Detailed player stats, trends and heat maps, plus exporting and importing your history as an Excel file. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

**Where is my game history stored?**
Only on your device. Oche has no accounts and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes your history. Use **Export** (Oche Pro) to keep a backup.

**How do I move my history to a new device?**
Export your history to Excel on the old device, move the file to the new one, and use **Import** in Oche.
