
# Oche

**Oche** is a darts score keeper for iPhone and iPad. Pick a game, add up to four players, and step up to the oche (the line you throw from). It keeps score, suggests checkouts, and saves every game so you can look back at your stats.

It is built with SwiftUI using Claude. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and it works fully offline.

## Features

### Games

- **X01**: 301, 501, or a custom start score. Choose double-out and double-in, and play first to 1 or more legs.
- **Cricket**: close 20 to 15 and the Bull. Turn on **Bull Worth 50** if the Bull should score 50 instead of 25.
- **Up to 4 players** per game. Drag to reorder, shuffle the throw order, rename players, and pick from names you have used before.
- **Two ways to enter scores in X01**: type the visit total, or **Tap Darts** to enter each dart (swipe up on a number for a double or treble). Busts are detected automatically in Tap Darts.
- **Checkout suggestions** show the finishing route when you are in range. You can turn this off.
- **Undo** the last throw or tap at any time. Leg and match wins, rematch, and "who throws first" are handled for you.

### Practice

- **Aim Trainer**: pick a target and see how often you hit it. Choose singles, doubles, and trebles, and set a number of visits or play unlimited.
- **Checkout Trainer**: you are given a score and have to finish it.
- **Around the Clock**: hit 1 to 20 in order.

### History and stats

- Every finished game is saved with its full throw-by-throw log.
- Filter history by game type. Rename or delete players and games.
- **Share Result** creates a result card you can send to friends.

### Oche Pro (one-time purchase)

Free: all games, practice modes, and history.

Pro unlocks:

- Detailed player stats, 3-dart average trends, aim hit-rate trends, and a dartboard heat map
- **Export to Excel** and **Import from Excel** for your history

It is a single purchase, not a subscription. Use **Restore Purchases** on the Pro screen if you reinstall or switch devices.

### Look and feel

Light or dark, with haptic feedback on key controls.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-in and nothing to register.

**Does it need internet?**
No. The only time Oche talks to the App Store is for the Pro purchase and for restoring it.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your history, so export your history to Excel (Pro) if you want a backup.

**Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?**
Not at the moment. To move your history, export it to Excel on one device and import it on the other (Pro).

**Does it collect any data or show ads?**
No. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**How many players can I add?**
Up to four in X01 and Cricket. The practice modes are single-player.

**Can I use it for Cricket and X01 in the same session?**
Yes. Start a new game from the home screen and pick a different format. Each game is saved separately.

**What if I made a mistake mid-leg?**
Use **Undo** to step back one throw, or one dart in Tap Darts.

**I bought Pro but it's locked.**
Open the Pro screen, tap **Restore Purchases**, and make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

**Is there a subscription?**
No. Oche Pro is paid once.

**Can I use it on a Mac or Apple Vision Pro?**
It is built for iPhone and iPad. The App Store may also let you run iPhone and iPad apps on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro, but the layout has not been tested there.

**How do I report a bug or suggest something?**
See the [support page](index.md).
